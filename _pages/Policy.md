<h2 class="policy-section-title">Selected Policy Projects</h2>

<div class="policy-project">

<h3>Effects of the Statutory Minimum Wage on Poverty Risk, Incomes, and Public Finances</h3>

<div class="policy-meta">
<span class="policy-period">2026–2027</span> · In partnership with IAW Tübingen<br>
Commissioned by the Federal Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (BAuA) ·
<a href="https://www.rwi-essen.de/rwi/team/person/projekte/detail/auswirkungen-des-gesetzlichen-mindestlohns-auf-die-armutsgefaehrdung-452" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Project page</a>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Description</summary>
<p>Evaluation of the effects of the 2022 increase in the German statutory minimum wage to €12 on poverty risk, household incomes, working hours, and public finances, using data from the German Socio-Economic Panel (SOEP) and a microsimulation model.</p>
</details>

</div>

<div class="policy-project">

<h3>Consultancy Services for Modelling and Simulations in Indonesia</h3>

<div class="policy-meta">
Commissioned by Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) GmbH ·
<a href="https://www.rwi-essen.de/en/rwi/team/person/projects/detail/consultancy-services-for-modelling-and-simulations-of-471" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Project page</a>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Description</summary>
<p>Further development of the Indonesian Ministry of Finance's existing microsimulation models for corporate income tax (CIT) and personal income tax (PIT).</p>
</details>

</div>

<div class="policy-project">

<h3>Joint Economic Forecast (Gemeinschaftsdiagnose)</h3>

<div class="policy-meta">
Biannual · Joint project of Germany's leading economic research institutes<br>
Commissioned by the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy ·
<a href="https://gemeinschaftsdiagnose.de/2025/09/25/gemeinschaftsdiagnose-herbst-2025-expansive-finanzpolitik-kaschiert-wachstumsschwaeche/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Autumn 2025 report</a> ·
<a href="https://gemeinschaftsdiagnose.de/2025/04/10/gemeinschaftsdiagnose-fruehjahr-2025-geopolitischer-umbruch-verschaerft-krise-strukturreformen-noch-dringlicher/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Spring 2025 report</a>
</div>

<details>
<summary>Description</summary>
<p>Contribution to labour market forecasts covering employment, unemployment, and wages as part of the Joint Economic Forecast. The Autumn 2025 report examined how expansionary fiscal policy masks weak growth, while the Spring 2025 report addressed the economic implications of geopolitical upheaval and the need for structural reforms.</p>
</details>

</div>

<h3>Study on the Payment Attitudes of Consumers in the Euro Area (SPACE)</h3>

<div class="policy-meta">
<span class="policy-period">2022–2023</span> · European Central Bank (ECB) ·
<a href="https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/ecb.spacereport202212~783ffdf46e.en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ECB SPACE report</a>
</div>
